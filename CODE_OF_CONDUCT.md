# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other people's privacy and safety.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is publicly
representing the project, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and related
community discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainer. If the report must
remain confidential, use the repository's private reporting form and begin the
title with `[Conduct]`:

https://github.com/EmilsValdmanis/omarchy-localhost/security/advisories/new

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as privately as practical. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other material that
violates this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
